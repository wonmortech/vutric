Design system v0.1 · Phase 0 · 17 November 2026

Vutric design system for phone-to-desktop transfer

Colours, type, controls and every first-release screen of Vutric: flick a photo off your Android phone and it flies onto your Windows or Ubuntu desktop.

See the screens Watch the effect

What Vutric does. Everything it can do today, and everything on the way — honestly labelled.

01

Offline. No cloud, no account.

Files travel inside your own Wi-Fi, encrypted end to end. Nothing needs the internet; nothing is uploaded to anyone.

02

You throw it.

Flick a photo off the top edge of the phone and it flies through a glowing portal onto the computer — the gesture is the product.

03

Any device, any file.

Phone→desktop, phone→phone, desktop→desktop. Photos, videos, documents, folders, clipboard — many at once, resumed if the link drops.

Status: Built Verifying on devices Soon · P1–P5 Opt-in Watched Not possible

One honest line: no real-world Wi-Fi transfer speed is published yet — every speed figure so far is a loopback test. The only live, production-representative number is the overlay effect’s 60 FPS. Anything marked Verifying on devices is built and tested in code, not yet run on a real phone, relay or hotel Wi-Fi.

How it connects. One protocol, many ways to reach it. Vutric tries them in order, automatically.

  1. 1Same Wi-Fi mDNS, then the addresses in the QR
  2. 2Direct over the internet hole-punching
  3. 3Our own relay when the network blocks direct
  4. 4Offer a hotspot, Wi-Fi Aware or a cable when there is no shared network

Phase 5

Private by design. Encrypted, device-to-device, and careful about who may send to you.

Trusted devices only Built

After the first QR, both devices remember each other’s key. Who may reach you is off, trusted only (default), everyone for 10 minutes, or while pairing.

Six-digit check Built

Pairing shows the same six digits on both screens, derived from the encrypted session — it cannot be faked by a device in the middle.

Nothing rich before you’re trusted Built

Before a device is admitted, only tiny fixed messages are read — no filenames, no previews, nothing that could be exploited.

Every parser is fuzzed Built

Seventeen fuzz targets hammer every format Vutric reads. The first run already found and fixed three real bugs.

The relay is blind Verifying

When a relay is used, its operator sees only keys, addresses and byte-counts — never your names, files or content.

Your device name stays private Built

The name travels only inside the encrypted session. The lookup that finds you carries a key, not a name.

Honest about what can’t be done

  • AirDrop is closed to other apps — but an iPhone can send through our browser page, or install Vutric. Not possible
  • Apple Continuity / Universal Clipboard is closed. Not possible
  • Samsung / Huawei / Mi Share are brand-only. Their Wi-Fi Direct idea is our R7. Not possible
  • Xender / SHAREit / Zapya use closed protocols. Their “phone opens a hotspot” idea is our R3. Not possible
  • Phone Link / Windows Nearby Sharing are closed — the Vutric desktop app plays that role. Not possible

Where files land, and how. One set of rules, the same on every device, every one overridable.

Your folder, your way

Pick the save folder; split by type into sub-folders; keep or flatten the sender’s folders; add a copy to the gallery.

When a name clashes

Keep both, replace, skip — or be asked each time.

Accept automatically

Ask every time, auto-accept from trusted devices, or auto-accept under a size you choose.

Safe with your disk

Refuses a transfer that would leave under 1 GB free, on both the target and the app’s own storage.

History & resend

A history of what went where, with progress and a one-tap resend.

Big files, handled

Many files in one go, up to four at a time; a dropped link resumes from where it stopped; every file is hash-checked before it is kept.

Settings have one owner inside the app, with a versioned format that migrates cleanly — so a new version never loses your choices. Built   The on-screen settings for desktop are Soon · P1.

When things go wrong. Every failure has a clear answer — that is part of the product, not an afterthought.

  • Connection lost“Reconnect”, and nothing half-saved is kept.
  • They said noA clear “rejected”, with how to pair or ask them to open up for 10 minutes.
  • No answerA timeout, not a hang.
  • The Wi-Fi keeps devices apartDetected, with the relay or a hotspot offered.
  • Hotel login wallNamed, with the sign-in link and a cable hint.
  • iPhone blocks the local networkA screen that explains the permission.
  • Low disk spaceRefused before it starts, not half-way.
  • A corrupt transferCaught by the hash check and retried.
  • An old pairing QRRejected — “scan the QR again”.
  • A stranger during pairingTreated as a guest: every file is asked about.
