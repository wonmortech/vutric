Halqa, the default effect

  1. Open0–250 msThe portal grows with a small overshoot.
  2. Fly105–650 msThe photo follows your throw: speed sets depth, angle bends the path.
  3. Land650 ms →A spring settles scale and tilt.
  4. Hold1,4 sThe photo rests where it landed.
  5. Dock450 msIt shrinks into the notification corner.

Measured on an Intel UHD 730 at 2560×1440: 60 fps, first frame under 11 ms, keyboard focus never taken.
